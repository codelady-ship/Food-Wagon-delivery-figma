About Food Wagon

A delivery app, built end to end.

Food Wagon is a demo food delivery service for Baku, Azerbaijan. It connects customers with 8 sample local restaurants across Italian, burger and grill, Asian, chicken, healthy, dessert and drinks menus.

The site is a full-stack portfolio project: a React and TypeScript front end with a Node.js, Express and MongoDB API for restaurants, menus, search, carts, orders, accounts, reviews, favourites, promo codes and an admin panel.

Delivery fees are $1–$3 per order and free from $30. Typical delivery time is 15–40 minutes. Nothing is really delivered or charged.

FAQ

Good questions.

Still curious? Write to us.

What is Food Wagon?+

Food Wagon is a demo food delivery web app for Baku. You can browse 8 sample restaurants, add dishes to a cart, check out and track the order. It is a portfolio project: no real food is delivered and no money is charged.

How long does delivery take?+

Each restaurant shows its own estimate, between 15 and 40 minutes. After checkout the tracking page shows each step: order received, preparing, on the way and delivered.

How much does delivery cost?+

Delivery costs $1 to $3 depending on the restaurant and is free when your subtotal is $30 or more. Pickup orders have no delivery fee.

Which payment methods can I use?+

You can choose a demo card payment or cash on delivery. Because this is a demo, no card details are collected and no payment is processed.

How do I track my order?+

Every order gets a reference such as FW-1A2B3C. Enter it on the Track order page, or open My orders in your account, to see the live status.

Are there promo codes?+

Yes. WELCOME10 gives 10% off orders over $15, FREESHIP gives free delivery over $10 and BIG5 takes $5 off orders over $40. Enter the code in your cart.

Do I need an account to order?+

No. Guests can order with a name and phone number. A free account adds order history, favourite restaurants and the option to write reviews.

How can a restaurant join Food Wagon?+

Send the Become a partner form on the Contact page with your restaurant name and cuisine, and the team replies within one working day.
